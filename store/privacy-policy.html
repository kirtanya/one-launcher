One Launcher

Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

In short: One Launcher does not collect, store on any server, or share any of your data. It has no ads, no analytics and no accounts, and no server of its own. What you type in search stays on your phone. Its only connections are to your own TV on your Wi-Fi (if you use the TV remote) and, for music controls, the cover picture the playing app points to.

What the app reads, and why

To work as your home screen, One Launcher reads some information on your phone. It is used only on the phone, only to show it to you, and is never sent anywhere.

  • Your installed apps (names and icons) — to show and sort them into folders.
  • App usage time (only if you allow Usage access) — to show today's screen time on the Screen time page.
  • Calendar events (only if you allow Calendar access) — to show your next event on the Up next page.
  • What's playing, and how many notifications each app has (only if you allow Notification access) — to show music controls on the Now playing page, and badges and live tiles in the Tiles view. The content of your notifications is not read or saved.
  • Mindful open (only for apps you choose) — those apps open from the launcher only on a fast double or triple tap, to cut down on habit opens. The list and your PIN stay on your phone.
  • A photo you pick (only if you choose "Your photo" as the background) — a copy is kept inside the app to draw your wallpaper.

What the app saves

Your settings (layout, folders, pinned apps, colours and so on) and how often you open apps from the launcher are saved privately on your phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

If you use Backup & restore, the app saves your settings to a file in a place you choose. That file is yours; the app does not send it anywhere.

Music cover pictures

Some music and video apps don't hand over the cover picture itself, only a web address for it. One Launcher then downloads that picture from that address to show it with the music controls. Nothing about you is sent with it.

Search

Search looks only through what's on your phone (apps, settings, your recent searches). "Search Google" and "Search Play Store" hand what you typed to those apps only when you tap them.

The TV remote

If you pair a TV, One Launcher talks directly to that TV over your local Wi-Fi to send button presses (and receives its name and volume level). The TV's address and a pairing key stay on your phone. Nothing goes over the internet.

Sharing

None. One Launcher sends none of your data to any server. There are no third-party services, ads or trackers in the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes
