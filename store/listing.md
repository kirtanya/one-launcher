## Full description (max 4000)

One Launcher puts your whole phone on a single, calm screen. Your clock and glanceable info sit on top, your apps sit below — swipe up for the full list. No pages to flip through, no clutter.

ONE SCREEN, EVERYTHING IN REACH
• A big, beautiful clock up top, with your apps right underneath
• Swipe sideways at the top for Screen time, Battery, Up next from your calendar, Now playing music controls, your favourite app widgets — or just your wallpaper
• Choose how much room the top gets, and how much padding around it

APPS THAT SORT THEMSELVES
• Smart folders group your apps automatically: Social, Work, Money, Watch & Listen, Shopping, Travel and more
• Open one folder at a time; it glides to the middle so it's always in focus
• Optional: folders open on their own as you scroll past them
• Rename folders, choose their apps, move apps between them
• A green card up top holds your pinned apps and the ones you use most

FIND ANYTHING FAST
• Search sits at the bottom, right under your thumb
• Type a few letters; if there's only one match, it opens straight away (you can turn this off)

QUICK ACTIONS
• One-tap chips above your apps: timer, flashlight, camera, calculator, alarms and more
• Add shortcuts from your apps, like scanning a QR code or writing a new email
• Style the chips your way: colour, transparency, shape, size, icon or label

YOUR COLOURS, YOUR WALLPAPER
• Follows your wallpaper's colours (Material You), or pick your own colour and style
• Light, dark or automatic cards
• A clock font you tune yourself: weight, width and slant
• Dim your wallpaper for readability, or use any photo as your background — move, zoom and blur it to fit

TV REMOTE
• Control your Google TV or Android TV from the dock: D-pad, volume, play/pause, power, and open YouTube, Netflix and more
• Pair once with the code on your TV; works on your home Wi-Fi

MADE FOR EVERY PHONE
• Work profile apps included
• Lighter effects switch on automatically on budget phones, so it stays smooth everywhere
• Back up all your settings to a file and restore them on a new phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no tracking, no accounts
• Nothing you do leaves your phone: the only connection it ever makes is to your own TV on your Wi-Fi, for the remote

Set One Launcher as your home app in Settings to get started.
